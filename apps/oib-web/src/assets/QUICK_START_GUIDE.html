Depot Readiness AI — Quick Start Guide

Use this guide for a self-paced orientation or as a printable leave-behind.

1. Readiness Command

Start here. Review the integrated readiness score and the largest drivers across equipment, workforce, supply, production, and quality.

2. Asset Reliability AI

Investigate equipment health, predicted failure risk, and maintenance priorities.

3. Workforce AI

Review available capacity, skill/certification gaps, training due, and OpEx-enabled actions such as cross-training and shift rebalancing.

4. Supply AI

Identify inventory shortages, long lead times, source concentration, and parts likely to constrain maintenance or production.

5. Production Flow AI

Compare planned and forecast output. Identify work centers and constraints reducing throughput.

6. Quality AI

Review yield, defects, NCR patterns, and quality signals that may create rework or schedule risk.

7. OIB Data Readiness & Connectivity Assessment

During a depot walk-through, enter connectivity, protocol, Government data-rights, cyber status, history, and data-quality information. The prototype scores each asset Green, Yellow, or Red and recommends the next integration action.

8. Data Provenance

Use this page to explain which structures are based on public reference sources and which records are synthetic demonstration data.
