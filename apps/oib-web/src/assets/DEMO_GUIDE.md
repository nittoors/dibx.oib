# 8-minute Demo Guide

1. **Readiness Command:** explain that readiness is decomposed across asset, workforce, supply, production and quality drivers.
2. **Asset Reliability AI:** show that the same health framework spans engines, CNCs, cranes, presses and infrastructure.
3. **Workforce AI:** show skill/certification coverage and surge planning, not employee ranking.
4. **Supply AI:** show single-source/lead-time/inventory interactions.
5. **Production Flow AI:** explain cross-domain bottleneck reasoning.
6. **Quality AI:** show recurring defect pattern concept.
7. **Data Readiness Assessment:** enter a real or hypothetical asset, score it, and export CSV/JSON.
8. **Provenance:** explain exactly which public sources informed the synthetic model and what remains synthetic.

Core message: `Depot Readiness AI is not another dashboard. It connects the operational causes of readiness risk and gives leaders actionable, explainable recommendations.`
